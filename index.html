Glossary of Terms

Udasi
A journey or pilgrimage, specifically referring to the five major journeys of Guru Nanak Dev Ji.
Janamsakhi
Biographical literature detailing the life of Guru Nanak Dev Ji.
Shabad
A hymn or sacred poem from the Guru Granth Sahib.
Gurdwara
A Sikh place of worship and community center.
Langar
The free community kitchen served at Gurdwaras, open to all visitors regardless of background.
Sat Sang
True congregation or holy company, referring to spiritual fellowship.

Teaching Resources

Discussion questions and activities for educators:

  • Discussion Questions:
    • How did Guru Nanak's travels challenge religious orthodoxy of his time?
    • What role did the concept of "Langar" play in promoting social equality?
    • How do the Udasis reflect the geographical and cultural diversity of the Indian subcontinent?
  • Activities:
    • Map tracing exercise: Have students trace one of Guru Nanak's journeys on a blank map.
    • Role-play activity: Students can role-play conversations between Guru Nanak and religious leaders he encountered.
    • Comparative analysis: Compare Guru Nanak's teachings with other contemporary religious leaders.
